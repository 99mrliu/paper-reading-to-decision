(paired 95% intervals excluding zero). Within a corpus it
stays within 0.001 of static interpolation everywhere, against
an oracle gain of 0.007–0.041 (Supplementary Table S11).
This is a negative result for one classifier and threshold
procedure on these features; it does not rule out a better
router, but it does mean the tax cannot be routed around with
the cheap signals we tested.
Table 11 shows why. The features that matter differ by
corpus: the dense top-1 margin dominates on SciFact, TREC-
COVID, and Quora, the BM25 WIG predictor on ArguAna
and SciDocs (and ranks second on FiQA), the score-ratio
α on FiQA, and centroid distance on NFCorpus; the mean
pairwise Spearman correlation between corpora’s importance
vectors is only 0.28. More damaging still, the direction of as-
sociation flips: 11 of the 17 features are positively associated
with failure on at least one corpus and negatively on another
(mean pairwise Spearman correlation of the association vec-
tors 0.33). The dense margin is the clearest case: a confident
dense top-1 predicts a safe query on the dense-dominated
corpora, where fusion can only dilute it, and a failing query
on TREC-COVID, where BM25 is equally strong and the
dense-heavy weight discards evidence. The same signal has

H. DOES RERANKING PAY THE TAX?
Table 13 reports the budget-matched reranking experiment
of Section IV-C. The answer is: partly, and only where the
reranker is itself reliable. On the three corpora where the
cross-encoder improves every retrieval arm (SciFact, FiQA,
TREC-COVID; dense-only rises by 0.019–0.142), reranking
lowers the prevalence of interpolation’s deployable tax by a
third on SciFact and not at all on FiQA and TREC-COVID,
where 16% and 22% of queries still finish below reranked
dense-only; for RRF the prevalence falls by 46% and 34% on
SciFact and FiQA and not at all on TREC-COVID. These are
net changes in how many queries pay the tax, not repairs of
the original failures: on SciFact 27 of the 30 originally hurt
queries are no longer hurt after reranking but 17 others be-
come hurt, on FiQA 75 of 95 are repaired and 81 newly hurt,
and on TREC-COVID 5 of 7 are repaired and 9 newly hurt
(Supplementary Table S12), so the reranker largely reshuffles
which queries pay the tax. On NFCorpus the reranker raises
dense-only by only 0.003 while lowering both fused arms (in-
